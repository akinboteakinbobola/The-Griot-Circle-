About The Griot

📍 Lagos, Nigeria • Akinbobola Akinbote Ambrose | 2026 • Culture Keeper

I am Akinbobola Akinbote Ambrose, storyteller, culture keeper, and founder of The Griot Circle from Lagos, Nigeria.

My mission is simple: Keep African stories alive in digital age. Our grandparents told us stories by moonlight. Today, moonlight is phone screen — but story must continue.

Every tale, proverb and history on this site is collected, verified, and shared with love for our heritage. If you have a story from your village, share it with me.
