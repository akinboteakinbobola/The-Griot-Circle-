The Griot — Author Story

Akinbobola Akinbote

Cultural historian, food anthropologist & storyteller. I am documenting Yoruba heritage, Ijebu origin, Nigerian food systems (Garri, Fufu, Zobo), and the hidden African roots of global faith — from Ojuelegba Shrine to Nsude Pyramids, from Tinubu Square to the Bible.

Mission: To reclaim our stories before they are licked like ice cream. A worker's sweat is not ice cream. Every thread you read here connects Lagos to the world.
